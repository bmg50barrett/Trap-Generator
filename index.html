How It Works

The "Click" rule. When the characters encounter a trap, the DM declares "CLICK!" Going around the table, the players say how their character reacts. That decision confers a bonus or penalty on the saving throw.

Reactions. Brace, Jump, or Prone. Riskier choices give a greater chance of advantage with an equal risk of disadvantage. Prone is the riskiest option, Jump is the median, and Brace is the safest.

Saves. A good reaction for the trap gives advantage. A bad reaction gives disadvantage. Any other reaction rolls normally. A "-" in the table means no reaction is good (or bad) for that trap.

Lasting conditions. Failing the save by 5 or more adds a condition that lasts until a Long Rest or until corrected.
